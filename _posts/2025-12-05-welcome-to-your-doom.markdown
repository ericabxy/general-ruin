General Ruin is a blog and knowledge base documenting the open source Doom family of engines with respect to independent game and level design.

Doom is a unique game engine designed for modding, but comes with many limitations that can restrict the imagination of a game designer. Nonetheless, much is possible both with the [original engine][1] and its most [faithful successors][2]. Furthermore, a number of generalizations are possible with careful modification of the source code.
